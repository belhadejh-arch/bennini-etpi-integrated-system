---
name: Field expense workflow
description: The one-record submission and administrative review workflow for expenses entered from the field.
---

Field staff submit expenses and their supporting details directly to the shared system; administration reviews that same submission. Do not require accounting staff to re-enter field expenses as a second transaction.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires direct synchronization to the main platform so that employees do not enter the same operation twice.

**How to apply:** Keep field submissions visible to administration with the original submitter, timestamp, notes, and documents. When changing summaries or review flows, preserve the original record and avoid counting it a second time.

New spare-part and repair operations belong only in the foreman's field workflow, with a required registered machine, not in a separate spare-parts workspace. Preserve historical parts records and their costs.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested removing the independent spare-parts section and having machine details and parts/repair counters reflect field operations.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce a second entry workflow. Keep legacy records readable without duplicating them as new field expenses.
