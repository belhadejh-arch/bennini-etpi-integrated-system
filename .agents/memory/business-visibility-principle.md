---
name: Business visibility principle
description: The management system should answer the owner's operational and financial questions through a clear, accessible interface.
---

Build the system as a practical management tool, not merely a collection of data-entry tables. The owner should be able to easily see where money went, income and expenses, purchases, inventory and its value, expected profits, due cheques, active rentals, machinery and its related costs, field-manager submissions, who recorded each operation and when, and who is allowed to access each part of the data. Present these answers through a simple, clear interface.

**Why:** the user stated this as a very important principle for the entire system.

**How to apply:** when designing or changing workflows, dashboards, reports, and permissions, check that relevant information is easy to find and attributable to the responsible person and time. Do not reduce the experience to raw tables or add unsupported totals in place of real data.
