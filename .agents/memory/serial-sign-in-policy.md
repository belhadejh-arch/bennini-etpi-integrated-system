---
name: Serial sign-in policy
description: The user's chosen policy for signing in with assigned serial codes.
---

Do not throttle or temporarily lock sign-in attempts for assigned serial codes. Keep validating the serial, account status, and the member's normal permissions.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose to remove attempt limits and blocking so members can enter easily with their serial code.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce failed-attempt limits or temporary login lockouts unless the user asks to change this policy.
